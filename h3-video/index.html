H3 Video

Fast text-to-video passes for people who iterate a lot

H3 Video is a text-to-video tool built around speed rather than maximum fidelity. You write a prompt, pick an aspect ratio, and get a short clip back in well under a minute on a normal connection. That speed changes how you work: instead of writing one careful prompt and waiting, you write five rough ones and keep the take that has the right motion.

The output is best described as concept-grade. Faces hold up at medium distance, camera moves are readable, and the model handles simple physics like falling objects or water without obvious artefacts. Close-ups and hands are still the weak point, which is true of nearly every model in this price bracket.

Where it earns a place in a workflow is pre-visualisation. Storyboard frames are cheap; motion tests are not. Generating ten variations of a shot before committing to a heavier model saves both credits and time, and the visual language you settle on here carries over.

Where it fits

The tool is listed at h3video.org. Notes here cover the parts that documentation tends to skip: cost per usable clip, the failure cases, and the point where a different model is the better choice.
